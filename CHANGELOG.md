# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Mouse camera and screen correction (v0.61): left click picks, left drag orbits (beyond 5 px, the release keeps the selection), right drag dollies (up = closer, the lens is untouched), middle or Shift+right drag pans, and the wheel zooms toward the model surface under the cursor (that point stays put on screen). A camera VMD is released only by real camera movement and hands over its position, direction, screen tilt (roll) and field of view; "수평 맞추기" removes the tilt. Drags end on focus loss. The inspector's "화면 보정" gives the selected character or background a colour (R/G/B) and brightness, and in "재질별 보정" one real PMX material (picked from the list or by clicking it in the viewport) its own colour, brightness and gloss (toon highlight or PBR smoothness; shown as unsupported on the original style). Corrections are drawn through per-material property blocks (the PMX, textures and materials are untouched), saved with the scene, undoable, compared before/after with one button, used by preview and output alike, and kept but not applied when the material can no longer be identified (index and name, or a unique name). Undo now re-applies only what the command changed.
- Lip sync and VRM research (D-02, D-04): `docs/D02_LIPSYNC_RESEARCH.md` compares signal processing, lyrics+timing and ML approaches and measures an LPC vowel classifier on synthetic vowels (99%/90% clean, 74%/38% with accompaniment at 20 dB) - a vocal-only track is effectively required; `docs/D04_VRM_RESEARCH.md` covers runtime import with UniVRM, the licence metadata display, MMD-to-humanoid bone mapping with pose correction, MToon options and distribution, recommending a separate ticket.
- Motion fit diagnosis (D-03, part 1): the motion section lists what of the VMD the model cannot show, in the user's words: missing semi-standard bones with the symptom each causes, other missing bones grouped, missing mouth and eye morphs counted. Runtime completion of semi-standard bones and foot grounding are investigated in `docs/D03_MOTION_FIT.md`.
- Beat-based automatic camera (D-01): "자동 카메라 (박자)" analyses the scene audio locally (spectral flux, comb tempo search with octave checks, kick-band phase, least-squares beat refinement) and generates a camera VMD whose cuts fall on bar lines: front full-body, upper-body, close-up and slow-orbit shots framed on the chosen character, chosen by a seeded generator weighted by section energy, with optional per-beat shake. The VMD is saved next to the scene, connected as the camera motion (undoable) and the generator version, inputs and seed are stored with the scene. Known-tempo tracks are found within 0.06 BPM and 12 ms; the real test song reads 126.3 BPM; cuts are on beats, the character's head stays in frame, and the same seed gives the same file.
- Output (H3-01..04): "출력" renders PNG (current frame), MP4 (H.264 + AAC through Windows Media Foundation, own plugin `EdsMedia.dll`, no bundled codecs) and transparent PNG sequences (characters only, straight alpha) at 1920x1080, 1080x1920, 1080x1080 or a custom size, 30/60 fps (60 default). Frame times come from integer ticks; frames are rendered off-screen after the physics bake, independent of the window and frame rate; audio is interleaved per frame and measured in sync within 0.1 ms. Files are written under a temporary name and replaced only when complete; cancel and failures leave existing files and no temporaries. Portrait/square guides show the output area and framing fits inside it. A real 10 s 1080p60 export with music took 5.2 s on the development PC.
- Material styles (H2b-01): per object "원본" (imported), "툰" (own URP toon shader: light/shadow border with position and strength, rim light, outline where the PMX draws edges, receives and casts shadows) and "PBR" for backgrounds (smoothness, metallic). The imported materials are kept and restored instantly; draw order, double-sided and transparent settings follow the original; unmapped materials stay original with a note. Saved per object, undoable, kept on relink; look presets now give characters the toon style. Shaders ship from Resources (15 and 13 variants). Fixed a D3D12 crash at exit caused by an unused second texture in the toon shader; verify-all now fails when a Player crashes while quitting.
- Look presets (H2a-05): "룩 프리셋" applies light, shadows, post-processing and background in one undoable step (camera, saved views, focus target and effect quality stay). Editing a value afterwards shows "(수정됨)" with "다시 적용". User presets can be saved (overwrite by name), deleted, and live in the app settings folder. Scenes store the preset name next to the actual values, so a preset changed later does not change a saved scene. Three proposed built-ins (bright stage, evening outdoor, dark club) await the user's reference images; with the models' unlit fallback shader the light colour does not tint the models yet (H2b).
- Camera VMD on the scene clock (H2a-04): connect a camera VMD in the camera inspector and follow it while playing, paused or seeking. Our own reader keeps the six interpolation curves separate and evaluates at any time (hard cuts kept); it matches UMT's reference path within 0.003 mm at all 2,737 keys of a real camera VMD. Manual navigation (orbit, pan, zoom, framing, view reset, saved views) takes over from the pose being shown and releases the motion; "카메라 모션 따라가기" resumes it. Orthographic keys, light/self-shadow keys and bone/morph keys in the file are reported and ignored; files without camera keys are rejected. Connect/remove are undoable; saved with the scene, missing files kept as references.
- Depth of field, SSAO, vignette and effect quality (H2a-02): the graphics section adds SSAO (intensity, radius), depth of field focused on a chosen character or a fixed distance (strength), vignette, a low/medium/high quality setting, "효과 초기화" (post-processing only) and "조명 초기화". All undoable, saved with the scene and restored on relaunch. SSAO now works on the models' unlit shaders (normals from depth, composited after opaques) and is off by default; the template had left it always on. Decision 9 met on the development PC: +2-3% frame time, +5-7% 1080p render cost.
- Scene audio (H2a-03): connect one WAV per scene from "환경 > 오디오" (PCM 8/16/24/32-bit or float, plain or extensible; compressed WAV is rejected with a message). It follows the common scene clock: seeks set the sample position (`round(seconds × rate)`), playback re-positions only past 80 ms drift, loop restarts it, the scene length covers the audio. Speeds other than 1x change the pitch and the playback bar says so. Volume and mute; connect/remove are undoable; the scene file stores the path (relative when possible), volume and mute in an additive `audio` block, and a missing WAV stays as a reference until replaced. Release IL2CPP: 155 UI and 10 restart checks.
- Neutral volume defaults (H2a-01): the URP assets no longer use the template's sample profile (bloom, vignette, tonemapping, motion blur); an editor step normalizes the defaults and the build fails if they drift. At runtime the app only sets its own effects. Same reference frames before and after; bloom and tonemapping verified in the rendered Player frame.
- Structure cleanup, second pass (H1-04): viewport input rules live in `ViewportInputRouter` (tested on its own with a fake target) and the selection highlight in `SelectionHighlight`. Debug IL2CPP passes the full regression suite.
- Structure cleanup, first pass (H1-04): repeated builds no longer rewrite the tracked smoke scene; `scripts/bash/verify-all.sh` runs the whole scene regression suite (UI, restore, format, open cancel/retry, crash recovery) and prints one summary; the scene workspace is split into input, selection, import and remove parts; file dialogs sit behind an interface so tests press the real toolbar buttons. The delete prompt no longer claims undo is unsupported.
- Diagnostics (H1-08): "진단" in the top bar shows version, build, backend, OS, CPU, GPU, display, a scene summary and recent errors/warnings. The text shown can be exported to a file, and the log folder can be opened. No model contents or machine name; the user profile folder is masked.
- Recent scenes (H1-07): a "최근 장면" toolbar button lists up to 10 recently opened or saved scenes, newest first. Missing files are marked and can be removed one by one or all at once; the list stores paths only, in the app settings folder.
- Relink missing files (H1-02): "파일 다시 찾기" in the inspector reloads a missing PMX from a new path with the same ID, placement, visibility, motion path and physics settings, in its document position; still-missing motions stay marked; undoable.
- Scene file schema 2 (H1-05): per-object physics, part strengths and body-motion preset live in a validated `physics` block. Schema 1 files open migrated in memory (the original is untouched until saved); newer versions are rejected. Save failures (read-only, permission, disk) show a clear message and keep the original and the unsaved state. Verified with schema 1 migration, Korean/space/special-character folders, another drive and a read-only file (`scripts/bash/run-scene-format.sh`); network paths not tested. Older builds cannot open schema 2 files.
- Undo/redo (H1-03): Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y and toolbar buttons, 200-command history. Placement, visibility, graphics, saved views, physics and body-motion settings, loop and speed are undone by re-applying only what differs; one gizmo drag is one command and quick repeated field/slider edits merge. Adding/removing objects and connecting a VMD are undone instantly by keeping the detached object or previous motion; returning to the saved point clears the unsaved mark.
- Autosave and crash recovery (H1-06): with unsaved changes the scene is written every 2 minutes to a separate recovery file (never the original); a normal save/new/open/quit removes it, and after a crash the next launch offers to restore (original path kept, marked unsaved), discard or decide later. Verified across forced kills (`scripts/bash/run-recovery.sh`).
- Body secondary motion (PH-05, ADR-018): chest/hip spring bones for models that have those bones but no rigid bodies, with detection in the inspector, presets (light/medium/strong), "apply to all characters", a model-terms notice and per-character saving. Spring bakes are deterministic and cheap (full song 4.3 s); in a chest close-up (forced test on Sandrone) the strong preset visibly moves the chest at the largest-swing moment; tuning on a real target model is still to do.
- Physics finish, part 1: compressed bake cache (Sandrone 158 → 71 MB, error at most 0.36 mm), jump check on visible bones only with an inspector warning, faster baking while paused (Sandrone full song in 132 s), and per-part physics strength sliders (hair, clothes, chest, hip, accessories, other; 0-100 %, instant, saved with the scene).
- Experimental per-character physics in the scene (off by default): the inspector's "물리 (실험)" toggle bakes physics ahead of playback, shows progress, re-bakes after motion or placement changes, falls back to off for that character on failure, and is saved per character (optional schema-1 field). Release IL2CPP and Release Mono pass 130 UI and 9 restart assertions.
- Physics bake cache engine (PH-03): stateful 60 Hz physics is computed ahead in per-frame slices and only physics-affected bones are cached and overlaid on the physics-OFF pose. It matches an uninterrupted simulation within 5.4 μm (Gene) and 0.7 μm (Sandrone); without restoring the solver state, the first tick after every bake-slice boundary is wrong (Gene: 91 of 1,201 ticks, up to 8.8 m), so the restore is required; the bake probe now compares every tick. Full-length bake: Gene 7.8 s / 30 MB, Sandrone about 185 s / 158 MB (not yet wired into the scene UI).
- PH-00 physics measurements (`scripts/bash/run-physics-measure.sh`): a 571-rigid-body model costs 11 ms per 60 Hz tick and 20 s to seek to 30 s, so the physics bake cache becomes the default playback path; chest and other secondary-motion bodies are stable, one model's cloak weight bodies are unstable, characters do not collide with each other.
- UnityMMDTools is now an embedded package (`MMDStudio/Packages/com.candidumgames.unitymmdtools`, base commit `db35d9cb`, local changes in `PATCHES.md`). Patch #1 releases VMD conversion native contexts and buffers when conversion throws: a failing conversion leaked 16.7 MB before and 407 bytes after (Release IL2CPP).
- Enhancement spec 0.5 (ADR-017): optional physics covering every PMX rigid body including body secondary motion, look presets, short-form/transparent output, and differentiation features (beat-based auto camera, lip sync, motion fitting).
- Added a repeated cancel/retry scene-open probe (`scripts/bash/run-scene-cycle.sh`): 20 Release IL2CPP cycles keep object counts identical and Unity allocated memory within +1.9 MiB.
- Loading progress callbacks can no longer abort PMX/VMD loading: an exception thrown from one is logged as a warning, so it cannot interrupt UMT's VMD conversion before it frees its native solver contexts.
- When UMT fails part way through building a model, the meshes, materials and avatar it already created are now destroyed instead of leaking; a fault-injection check in the PMX staging probe verifies nothing is left after failures in three late stages.
- Opening a `.mmdscene` no longer clears the current scene first: entries are prepared asynchronously as hidden candidates with a cancel button, and the scene is replaced in one step only when all are ready; cancel or failure keeps the running scene. ## v0.1.0-poc — 2026-11-19

Initial source checkpoint for Windows x64 feasibility work. This is not a completed stage-0 gate or the v1.0 MVP.

### Included

- Unity 6000.3.28f1 / URP project and pinned UnityMMDTools dependency.
- External PMX/VMD loading in Windows Player, a single-model UI Toolkit workspace, native Windows file selection and recoverable asset replacement.
- Direct Bezier/quaternion animation, controlled vertex morph/foot-IK checks, and limited camera VMD playback.
- Mono/IL2CPP build and verification scripts, native-library/notice inventory, and original external diagnostic-fixture generators.
- Physics endurance/reference investigations and a project-owned typed stateful solver/60 Hz lifecycle service. Physics is diagnostic/opt-in; the normal preview remains physics OFF.
- Requirements, architecture/decision records, source provenance/notices, and documented verification results/limits.

### Verification recorded before this checkpoint

- POC-06G: six lifecycle cases per backend, 20 assertions each, on actual Mono and IL2CPP Windows Players. Each backend has 179,712 bone-pose comparisons with zero reported difference from its own retained-state diagnostic reference.
- New Mono Player: 13 import/recovery and 21 expression/controlled IK regression assertions pass.
- Both POC-06G outputs pass native x64/required-export/notice inventory. Earlier reference and loader validation are detailed in docs/TASKS.md.
- Runtime test output is intentionally not tracked. Results here describe local verification; fresh clones must supply external assets and reproduce the checks.

### Remaining

- Multi-character scene and background management, object editing, production viewport/graphics controls, and scene save/restore.
- Longer-motion and stronger IK/append/physics validation, scalable/cancellable seeks, broader malformed-input handling and clean-machine deployment validation.
- Audio synchronization and full MVP UI integration.

### Checkout requirements

Open `MMDStudio/` with the pinned Unity Editor and restore UPM dependencies. PowerShell 7/Python 3 are used by verification scripts; Windows build modules, the relevant compiler toolchain and an activated Unity license are required for builds. Test models, motions, textures, builds, logs, research downloads, Python caches and machine-generated Unity folders are excluded from this repository.
